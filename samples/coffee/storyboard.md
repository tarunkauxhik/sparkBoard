# The Date on the Bag

## Concept
**The Date on the Bag** (score 35/50). One person in a kitchen hunts for a roast date on a generic bag and can't find one. They sigh, then pick up a Brasswick bag and point straight to the printed roast date. Voiceover or on-camera line: 'Whole beans, roast date on every bag. That's it. That's the flex.' Close on them grinding a few beans, with a text overlay: 'Brasswick small-batch roasted coffee. Monthly box.' The story is a brand that simply tells you when it roasted.
Why it won: The 'find the date' challenge is a strong scroll-stopper and puts the roast-date proof point front and center. It's held back by a text overlay and a label-reading close-up, both risky for AI video, and the 'that's the flex' line is a bit cute. The subscription is only a tag at the end.
