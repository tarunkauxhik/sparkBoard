# Drill vs. Deadline
Noctave Air wireless earbuds | product_hero | YouTube Shorts | US English | 12 s + end card (target 12 s)

## Concept
**Drill vs. Deadline** (score 42/50). One apartment desk. A loud drilling sound plays while the person winces at their laptop. They put in Noctave Air, tap to turn on active noise cancellation, the sound drops away, and they exhale and start typing. A beat later they pull one earbud out and the drilling comes back. They grin and put it back in. Voiceover: 'Active noise cancellation, for the noise you can't control.' Product hero close-up on the earbuds.
Why it won: A sound-led hook with a built-in on/off demo that proves ANC in seconds. It has one person, one desk, and simple actions, and the pull-out-and-grin beat is memorable and native to Shorts.
