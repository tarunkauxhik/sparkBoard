## Concept
**The Sting Test** (score 37/50). One woman in her bedroom, handheld to camera. She dabs Dewline on one cheek and narrates the wait in real time, with an on-screen counter ticking up to 10 seconds. She keeps checking and says 'nothing yet'. She mentions it's fragrance-free and made for sensitive skin, then admits she's still a bit cautious but this is the first time she hasn't flinched. Ends on a calm face and the bottle, with a line like 'Patch test it yourself.' No claims beyond the product facts.
Why it won: The real-time sting test turns the audience's biggest fear into the hook, and it feels native to Reels. The on-screen counter breaks the no-text rule for AI video, and 'first time I haven't flinched' edges toward a claim, though it stays mostly within the facts.

## Critic notes
- s01 (other): The hook says 'Serum on my cheek', which narrates what we already see and is a slow lead-in. It also contains no product claim, which is fine. Change: The hook now reads 'Waiting for the sting. Okay, nothing yet.' Wait, that adds a filler, so the line is 'Waiting for the sting, any second now.', which opens straight on the tension.
- s01 (micro_expression): The first frame relies on a set jaw and slightly raised shoulders to show she is braced, and that only reads if a tiny expression lands. Change: The first frame now has her gripping the bottle and holding the phone steady, with her free hand hovering near her cheek.
- s03 (micro_expression): The first frame relies on a relaxed mouth and lowered shoulders to show relief, which is a subtle expression. Change: The first frame now shows her turned cheek with the serum sheen and her hand resting on the sink edge.
- s04 (micro_expression): The first frame relies on relaxed shoulders and a calm cheek to show ease. Change: The first frame now shows her holding the bottle beside her chin with her free hand on the sink.
- s03 (claim): The line 'it's fragrance-free' is backed by product_facts, but it sounds like a spec-sheet point dropped in mid-check. Change: It is now phrased as 'Nothing yet... and it's fragrance-free, so that helps.', which keeps the backed claim in a more casual voice.
- end_card (ad_like): The end card line repeats the fragrance-free fact as a two-part feature list. Change: The end card line is now 'Made for sensitive skin.', one backed fact.
